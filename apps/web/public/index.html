> join omaglobe

Install the plugin: a globe in your omarchy bar that puts your machine on this map while it's online.

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/mark-omarov/omaglobe-plugin --enable
omarchy bar move io.github.mark-omarov.omaglobe --after omarchy.indicators

Hover the middle of your bar to find the globe, then switch it on. source

Already sharing? Open the map once from the globe (right-click it, or open the map) so this browser knows which dot is you. There are no accounts, so that's the only link.

> about

omaglobe shows omarchy machines that are online right now, by city. Each dot is someone who flipped a switch in their bar. Nobody is on here by accident.

what it knows

  • Your city and a random install id, while your plugin is on. We keep a hash of the id and forget it about an hour after the last check-in, or when you switch off.
  • Nothing else. No names, accounts, IP addresses, coordinates, cookies or analytics. This page shares nothing.
  • A city shows only once three machines are there, and the map moves once a minute, so nobody can tell when you joined.
