Only the city you pick is stored, never coordinates. A city shows on the map once 3 people share it, and the map updates once a minute so no one can tell when you joined. Stop sharing any time.

> omaglobe in your Omarchy bar

A globe next to the weather that puts your machine on this map while it's online. Off until you turn it on; only the city you pick is sent.

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/mark-omarov/omaglobe-plugin --enable

Pick the center section when asked. Then click the globe, choose your city and press Show me on the map. Right-click it to open this page in your current Omarchy theme.

> about omaglobe

An opt-in map of Omarchy users. A community project, not affiliated with Omarchy, Omacom or 37signals.

privacy

  • Nothing is collected unless you choose to share. Sharing is off by default.
  • Sharing from this page stores a random ID your browser makes (only its hash) and the city you picked. It's kept until you press stop, or 180 days after your last visit.
  • The Omarchy plugin sends a random install ID (stored hashed) and your city once an hour while it's on. It's dropped about an hour after the last one, or when you go offline.
  • No names, accounts, IP addresses, coordinates, cookies or analytics. A city shows only once 3 people are there, and the map updates once a minute so no one can tell when you joined.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and sees connections, as any host does.
